# Caccer Motorsports: design rules

Hand-picked cars in West Jordan, UT

Status: **proposed**. Bold and dark: big real car photos, black and red from the logo, yellow only for small badges, wide bold headings, Archivo Narrow for labels and specs.

## Color
| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| primary | `#c8102e` | buttons, links, key highlights |
| accent | `#ffd23f` | badges, underlines, small highlights; never body text |
| ink | `#111113` | body text |
| bg | `#ffffff` | page background |
| soft | `#f3f4f6` | cards, panels |
| dark | `#0b0b0d` | header, footer, dark sections |

Contrast: body text 18.86:1, white on primary 5.88:1, white on dark 19.66:1 (target 4.5:1 or more).

## Type
- Headings: **Archivo**
- Body and UI: **Archivo**
- Sizes: body 16px on web (never under 16px in form fields), 10.5 to 11pt in PDFs; headings 1.25 scale.

## Icons
One set only: **Tabler** (MIT). Brand logos (WhatsApp, Instagram, Zillow): Simple Icons.

## Do
- Real photos of the real work or product; one strong photo beats a collage.
- Generous white space, one primary button per screen, bilingual labels where clients choose a language.
- Keep Caccer's voice: confident, direct, no pressure; no financing language.

## Don't
- No stock-looking gradients or emoji as icons; no more than two fonts.
- Accent color is for highlights only, never long text.
